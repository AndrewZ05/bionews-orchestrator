# A Disciplined Approach to Identity
## Product Truth vs. Marketing Performance

---

### What Product Is Asking For

From the Identity Resolution & Enrichment Technical Specification:

- **Deterministic, auditable identity** — one official answer per person
- **Immutable history** — logic changes apply forward only, never retroactive
- **Explainable resolution** — every decision can be traced and justified
- **Centralized in the warehouse** — not in tags, vendors, or downstream tools
- **Safe for all stakeholders** — reporting, analytics, compliance, and clients

> **"This is our system of record."**

---

### What Marketing Needs

For revenue optimization and growth:

- **Better reach** — cross-device and cross-channel unification
- **Frequency control** — avoid duplicate touches to the same person
- **Improved attribution** — connect journeys across touchpoints
- **Audience expansion** — merge common identities for activation
- **Performance** — optimize campaigns, not just measure them

> **"This is about performance, not truth."**

---

### Why One Model Fails

| Approach | What It Solves | Where It Breaks |
|----------|---------------|-----------------|
| **Deterministic Only** | Stable, auditable, compliant | Limits reach, fragments audiences, constrains growth |
| **Probabilistic Only** | Maximum reach, better attribution | Rewrites history, breaks audits, erodes trust |

**The core tension:** The identity system Product needs cannot meet marketing's needs. The identity system marketing wants would break Product's guarantees.

---

### The Resolution: Layered Identity Architecture

**One foundation. Three layers. Clear boundaries.**

| Layer | Purpose | Who Uses It |
|-------|---------|-------------|
| **Evidence Ledger** | Immutable facts & signals | Foundation for all |
| **Deterministic Identity** | System of record | Reporting, Analytics, Clients |
| **Probabilistic Identity** | Revenue optimization | Marketing Activation only |

---

### The Key Principle

> **"We don't choose between accuracy and reach. We choose where each one belongs."**

- Deterministic truth where accuracy matters most
- Probabilistic power where revenue and growth matter most
- Each layer builds on the one below — none overwrite

---

### What This Enables

**For Product/Analytics:**
- Stable, reproducible metrics
- Auditable, explainable decisions
- Client trust preserved

**For Marketing:**
- Better reach and frequency control
- Improved attribution
- Revenue optimization without compliance risk

**For the Platform:**
- Foundation for a future CDP
- Scales safely as requirements evolve

---

*This identity architecture becomes the spine of a CDP — separating truth, learning, and activation so each can evolve safely.*
